# SFO Airport Pickup & Staging Procedures

**Purpose.** This policy governs Nexa Pulse operations at San Francisco International Airport (SFO), which operates under an airport TNC operating permit and its own ground-transportation rules.

**Staging and the FIFO queue.** Drivers waiting for SFO trip requests must wait inside a designated TNC staging lot within the airport's geofenced area. SFO uses a first-in, first-out (FIFO) queue: the `available` driver who has been in the staging area the longest receives the next request, regardless of physical position in the lot. Drivers who wait elsewhere on airport property are not added to the queue and risk citation. If all staging lots are full, drivers must leave airport property immediately. Staging lots carry a 60-minute time limit; the system should warn a driver at the 50-minute mark.

**Pickup and drop-off rules.** Pickups occur only at the designated rideshare pickup area (the Domestic Garage rideshare level); drivers must not pick up at arrivals/baggage-claim curbs except for riders with mobility needs where permitted. Drop-offs are at the departures level. Drivers must display a valid SFO placard and Nexa Pulse trade dress at all times on airport property and present a waybill on request.

**Fees.** SFO levies a per-trip fee on TNC trips that is passed through to the rider as a separate line item. The exact amount is a compliance-owned, configurable value and has varied over time; operations must use the current published SFO rate, not a hard-coded value. The California TNC Access for All fee of $0.10 per trip and the San Francisco congestion tax (where applicable) are handled separately under their own policies.

**Cancellations and short-trip handling.** Drivers who excessively cancel airport trips or ask riders to cancel may lose access to airport dispatch. After completing a very short airport-area trip, a driver may be eligible for a queue "short-trip bump" returning them near the front of the FIFO queue.

**Operations manager role.** The dashboard shows staging-lot occupancy, queue depth, and inbound flight banks. When `request_count` at SFO is forecast to spike (heavy arrival banks, especially during summer marine-layer fog delays that compress arrivals), the manager may issue repositioning guidance toward SFO under the Driver Repositioning & Incentive Program.
