# Fare Calculation, Adjustments & Refunds

**Purpose.** This policy defines how fares are computed, what fees are itemized, and how adjustments and refunds are authorized for San Francisco trips.

**Fare components.** A fare estimate (typically in the $8-$45 range for in-city trips) is built from a base fare plus time and distance along the computed route, any active surge multiplier, and itemized pass-through fees. Riders see an upfront fare or an estimate before confirming.

**Pass-through fees.** Trips originating in San Francisco are subject to the city's Traffic Congestion Mitigation Tax. Every completed California TNC trip carries the $0.10 Access for All fee. Trips touching SFO carry the airport's per-trip fee. These are displayed as separate line items, not folded silently into the base fare.

**Upfront vs. metered.** When the actual route materially differs from the estimate (rider adds stops, changes destination, or significant detour), the fare may be recalculated. Riders must be able to see why a fare changed.

**Adjustments and refunds.** Operations may issue an adjustment or refund when: a driver took an inefficient route not caused by traffic or rider request; a trip was charged despite a service-animal denial (full refund); a cancellation or wait-time fee was applied in error; or a documented service failure occurred. Refunds for erroneous fees should be processed promptly, with card refunds taking up to five business days.

**Damage and cleaning fees.** If a driver reports material damage or soiling, a cleaning/damage fee may be assessed up to the published maximum, subject to documentation (photos) and review; riders may dispute.

**Authorization holds.** A temporary authorization hold may appear at trip start and is voided automatically; it is not a charge. Operations should explain holds to riders who report duplicate charges.

**Controls.** Refund authority is tiered; large or unusual adjustments require supervisor approval and are logged for audit.
