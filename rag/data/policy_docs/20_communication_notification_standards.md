# Communication & Notification Standards

**Purpose.** This policy defines tone, channels, timing, and content standards for all Nexa Pulse communications with drivers and riders in San Francisco.

**Principles.** Communications must be clear, accurate, timely, and respectful. Operations advises and informs; it does not pressure drivers to drive at specific times or accept specific trips, a constraint required to preserve independent-contractor status under Proposition 22.

**Channels.** Operational messages — match confirmations, pickup updates, fee receipts, document-expiry reminders, and safety alerts — flow through the app and verified email/SMS. Marketing and promotional messages are separated from operational ones and honor opt-out. Standard carrier messaging rates apply to SMS.

**Pricing transparency.** Any surge or unusual pricing must be disclosed before the rider confirms. Fee line items must be itemized.

**Safety communications.** In an incident, messaging is concise and action-oriented: direct affected users to call 911 for emergencies first, then provide callback and follow-up. Safety callbacks target minutes for critical incidents and 24 hours for serious ones.

**Driver notifications.** Drivers receive proactive notice of: approaching hours-of-service limits, expiring documents, available incentives and priority zones, and account actions with reasons and appeal paths.

**Service disruptions.** During weather or major events, operations issues advance notice of expected demand, pickup-zone changes, and any safety guidance. Messages must never instruct unsafe or illegal behavior.

**Consistency and logging.** Templates are version-controlled and reviewed for accuracy and accessibility (plain language, screen-reader compatible). All outbound operational communications are logged for audit and dispute resolution.
