# Accessibility & Wheelchair-Accessible Vehicle (WAV) Policy

**Purpose.** This policy describes Nexa Pulse's commitments to riders with disabilities and the operational handling of wheelchair-accessible vehicle (WAV) requests in San Francisco. It is distinct from the Service Animal Policy, which addresses a separate legal obligation.

**Regulatory basis.** As a California TNC, Nexa Pulse complies with CPUC accessibility requirements and the TNC Access for All program established under SB 1376 (Hill, 2018) and implemented in CPUC Rulemaking 19-02-012. A $0.10 Access fee is collected on each completed TNC trip originating in California and remitted quarterly; eligible WAV investments may be offset against fees due. The app must allow riders to indicate access needs, including the need for a WAV, and must meet WCAG 2.0 AA accessibility standards.

**WAV dispatch.** Riders who require a non-folding wheelchair must be able to request a WAV. The operations dashboard tracks the count and status (`available`, `on_trip`, `offline`) of WAV-capable vehicles across the seven zones separately from the general fleet, because WAV supply is thin and demand is geographically dispersed. When a WAV request cannot be matched promptly, the operations manager should prioritize WAV repositioning incentives and may pre-schedule WAV trips where supported.

**Service standards.** WAV trips are priced using the same fare structure as standard trips of comparable distance; riders must not pay a premium for accessibility. Drivers operating WAVs must be trained in securement of mobility devices and in disability etiquette. Drivers must transport collapsible mobility devices (wheelchairs, walkers, crutches, canes) in any vehicle at no extra charge.

**Non-discrimination.** Refusing a rider because of a disability or assistive device is prohibited and is grounds for deactivation under the Driver Deactivation & Account Standards policy.

**Reporting.** Accessibility complaints are handled by a specialized team and tracked for the CPUC accessibility-plan section required in the annual report.
