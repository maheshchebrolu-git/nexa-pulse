# Driver Repositioning & Incentive Program

**Purpose.** This policy defines how Nexa Pulse encourages drivers with status `available` to relocate from over-supplied to under-supplied zones, and how repositioning incentives are structured. Participation by drivers is always voluntary; under Proposition 22, Nexa Pulse does not require drivers to accept any specific request or to be online at specific times.

**Demand signals.** The system continuously compares `request_count` against available-driver counts across the seven zones. Zones where open requests substantially exceed supply are designated "priority zones" and surfaced on the operations dashboard and the driver app map.

**Incentive types.** Two incentive structures are supported:

- **Zone bonuses**: a flat per-trip bonus (for example, $3-$8) added to a driver's earnings for completing a trip that originates in a priority zone during a defined window. The bonus "sticks" to the driver — once earned by entering the zone, it applies to the next trip even if the trip ends outside the zone.
- **Trip-count quests**: a bonus for completing a target number of trips within a time window (for example, $40 for 20 completed trips on a weekend evening), used to retain supply during sustained high demand.

**Operations manager role.** Nexa Pulse recommends which zones warrant incentives and at what level, based on the supply gap and the cost of unfilled `requested` trips. The manager approves, adjusts, or declines. Incentives should be wound down as available-driver counts recover to avoid paying for supply that is no longer scarce.

**Anti-abuse.** Incentives must not reward "dead-heading" without service. Repositioning bonuses are paid only on `completed` trips. The system flags drivers who repeatedly enter a priority zone and go `offline` without accepting requests.

**Guardrails.** Repositioning recommendations must never instruct a driver to make an unsafe or illegal maneuver, and must respect the fatigue limits in the Driver Fatigue & Hours-of-Service Policy. Repositioning guidance is advisory; drivers choose whether to follow it. Recommendations are framed as optional opportunities, never mandates, to preserve independent-contractor status under Proposition 22.
