# Lost & Found Procedures

**Purpose.** This policy defines how Nexa Pulse helps reunite riders with items left in vehicles after a trip is `completed`.

**Responsibility.** Riders are responsible for their belongings. Drivers are independent contractors and are not obligated to store or deliver lost items, and Nexa Pulse cannot guarantee recovery, provide reimbursement, or replace lost property. The platform's role is to facilitate contact.

**Rider-initiated flow.** A rider reports a lost item through the completed trip in their trip history, selecting "find lost item" and entering a callback number. The system connects the rider and driver through masked numbers. The rider should describe the item and, if the driver confirms it, arrange a mutually convenient meeting in a safe, public location.

**Return fee.** When an item is returned, a return fee is charged to the rider and passed in full to the driver to compensate for their time and mileage. The fee is waived in cases where the loss resulted from a platform or driver error.

**Driver-found flow.** A driver who finds an item should report it in the app and attempt return. Drivers should not open or use found property and should safeguard it until return is arranged or, if the rider cannot be reached, hand it to local authorities.

**High-value and sensitive items.** For IDs, passports, cash, payment cards, phones, or keys, riders should also take protective steps (remote-locate a phone, notify their bank). Items that cannot be returned within a reasonable period should be surfaced to support.

**Operations note.** Lost-item reports peak during late-night weekend operations in the Mission and Castro nightlife zones; staffing for Lost and Found follow-up should reflect this pattern. Lost-item cases are Tier 3 incidents under the Incident Response Protocol unless they reveal a safety concern.
