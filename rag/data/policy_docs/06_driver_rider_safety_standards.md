# Driver & Rider Safety Standards

**Purpose.** This policy sets the baseline behavioral and safety expectations for everyone on the Nexa Pulse platform in San Francisco.

**Driver conduct.** Drivers must hold a valid California driver's license, current registration, and required insurance, and operate a vehicle that passed the required inspection. Drivers must follow all traffic laws, use hands-free phone operation only, and obey SFMTA rules including prohibitions on double-parking, blocking bike lanes, and stopping in crosswalks. Off-platform pickups and street hails are prohibited; all trips must be prearranged through the app.

**Zero tolerance.** Nexa Pulse maintains a zero-tolerance policy for drug or alcohol use while a driver is `available`, `on_trip`, or otherwise using the platform. Any credible report of impairment results in immediate account hold and investigation under the Driver Deactivation & Account Standards policy.

**Prohibited behavior.** Discrimination, harassment, violence, sexual contact or misconduct of any kind, threatening or abusive language, and unauthorized post-trip contact are prohibited for both drivers and riders. Riders must not bring open containers of alcohol or illegal drugs into a vehicle, must not ask a driver to speed or make unsafe maneuvers, and are responsible for any car-seat requirements for children.

**Privacy of contact information.** Driver and rider phone numbers are masked through the app. Pickup and drop-off addresses are concealed in driver trip history after a trip is `completed`, consistent with the Data Privacy & Rider Information Handling policy.

**Recording.** Drivers may use a dashcam where lawful. California is a two-party-consent state, so riders should be notified that recording may occur.

**Reporting.** Both parties can report safety concerns in-app. Reports are triaged under the Incident Response Protocol. Patterns of dangerous-driving reports may lead to loss of platform access even where no single event violates the law.
