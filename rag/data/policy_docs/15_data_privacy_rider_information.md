# Data Privacy & Rider Information Handling

**Purpose.** This policy governs how Nexa Pulse collects, displays, retains, and shares personal data for riders and drivers in San Francisco.

**Data minimization.** Operations managers see only the data necessary to balance the marketplace: driver status, position, vehicle metadata, rating, and zone-level `request_count`. Access to full rider contact details and precise historical pickup/drop-off addresses is restricted and role-gated.

**Contact masking.** Rider and driver phone numbers are masked; in-app calling and messaging route through proxy numbers so neither party sees the other's real number. After a trip reaches `completed`, specific pickup and drop-off addresses are concealed in the driver's trip history.

**Location data.** Live driver positions and trip routes are used for matching, ETA, safety, and rebalancing. Location data is retained only as long as needed for operations, safety, dispute resolution, and regulatory reporting.

**Regulatory disclosure.** As a California TNC, Nexa Pulse submits annual trip-data reports to the CPUC under Decisions D.13-09-045 and D.16-04-041. Public versions of these reports must redact personally identifiable information — driver names and license numbers, vehicle identification numbers (VINs), and certain precise location and date fields must be aggregated or removed before any public disclosure.

**Law-enforcement requests.** Trip data is released to law enforcement only through the verified secure portal and only under valid legal process or genuine emergency, handled by the designated public-safety liaison. Operations managers must never share rider or driver data through informal channels.

**Recording and consent.** Where in-trip audio or video recording features exist, California's two-party-consent rule applies and users must be notified.

**Breach handling.** Any suspected data exposure is escalated immediately under the Operations Escalation Procedures.
