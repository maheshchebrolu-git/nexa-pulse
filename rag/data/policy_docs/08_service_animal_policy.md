# Service Animal Policy

**Purpose.** This policy states the absolute obligation of Nexa Pulse drivers to transport riders accompanied by service animals. It is deliberately separate from the WAV/Accessibility policy because the legal basis, the enforcement standard, and the operational handling differ.

**Legal standard.** Under the Americans with Disabilities Act and California law, a service animal is a dog (or in specific cases a miniature horse) individually trained to perform tasks for a person with a disability. Drivers may not deny a ride because of a service animal and may not charge any pet fee or extra cleaning fee for one. Allergies, religious objection, or general fear of dogs are **not** valid reasons to refuse; a driver who cannot transport service animals must not accept ride requests at all. A driver may ask only two questions: whether the animal is required because of a disability, and what task it is trained to perform.

**No exceptions.** The obligation applies to every vehicle type and every trip. Emotional-support animals are a separate category, but drivers should err toward acceptance and report concerns afterward rather than deny at pickup.

**Detection of violations.** Nexa Pulse flags a likely violation when a driver arrives at pickup and immediately cancels, and the rider then reports a service-animal denial. In such cases the rider is not charged a cancellation or no-show fee, and any erroneous fee is refunded (allow up to five business days). If a rider opts to notify drivers in advance that they travel with a service animal, a driver who tries to cancel at pickup receives an in-app reminder that refusal is unlawful.

**Enforcement.** A confirmed knowing refusal results in permanent deactivation under the Driver Deactivation & Account Standards policy. Repeated cancel-at-pickup patterns following service-animal disclosures are reviewed even absent a single confirmed report.

**Operations manager role.** Service-animal denial reports are high-priority and routed to the specialized accessibility/safety queue, not the standard complaint flow.
