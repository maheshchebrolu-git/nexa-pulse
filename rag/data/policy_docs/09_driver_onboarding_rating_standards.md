# Driver Onboarding & Rating Standards

**Purpose.** This policy defines how new drivers are vetted and admitted to the Nexa Pulse platform in San Francisco and how the rider-rating system governs ongoing eligibility.

**Onboarding requirements.** Before a driver can go `available` for the first time, they must complete: a national criminal and sex-offender database background check and a DMV driving-history check; verification of a valid California license, registration, and TNC-compliant insurance; and a 19-point vehicle safety inspection. Vehicles must seat no more than seven including the driver and must not be significantly modified from factory specification (no stretch vehicles). Drivers must complete the platform safety-and-conduct training before activation.

**Profile accuracy.** Each driver's stored metadata — name, phone, vehicle make, model, color, license plate, and rating — must be accurate. Riders rely on this data to identify their vehicle; providing inaccurate vehicle information is a violation. Driver ratings on the platform fall on a 4.0-5.0 scale.

**Rescreening.** Background and driving-history checks are rerun at least annually, and the system monitors for new citations or charges between checks. A disqualifying result discovered at any time triggers a hold under the Driver Deactivation & Account Standards policy.

**Rating standard.** Drivers must maintain a minimum average rating. A driver whose rolling average rating falls below 4.6 enters a coaching state; sustained ratings below the deactivation threshold (4.5) are grounds for review. Ratings are one input among several and are weighed against trip volume and the substance of rider feedback.

**Operations manager role.** The dashboard surfaces newly activated drivers, drivers approaching rating thresholds, and drivers with expiring documents (drivers cannot go `available` with an expired inspection, registration, or insurance). Managers monitor onboarding throughput against forecast supply needs by zone.
