# Major-Event Surge & Pre-Positioning Policy

**Purpose.** This policy governs pricing and supply planning around scheduled large-attendance events in San Francisco — Oracle Park and Chase Center games and concerts, Moscone Center conventions in SOMA, festivals in the Mission, and large gatherings in the Marina / Fisherman's Wharf tourist corridor. It is distinct from routine Dynamic Surge Pricing: event surge is *planned* and *forecast-driven* rather than purely reactive.

**Forecast and pre-positioning.** For each known event, the operations team builds a demand forecast 24-72 hours ahead, estimating the post-event `request_count` spike window (typically the 30-90 minutes after an event ends). Nexa Pulse recommends pre-positioning targets — a number of `available` drivers to stage near the venue zone before the surge begins — to soften the multiplier peak and reduce rider wait times.

**Pricing approach.** Because event demand is concentrated and predictable, the operations manager may pre-authorize a graduated surge schedule (for example, 1.5x at event end rising to a 2.5x ceiling if `request_count` exceeds forecast). Event surge ceilings may be set lower than routine surge ceilings when a venue has established curbside staging, because managed loading reduces pickup friction.

**Coordination.** Event operations require coordination with venue ground-transportation staff and, where the event affects road closures, the SFMTA. Designated event pickup zones must be communicated to drivers in advance. Drivers asked to pre-position should be offered an incentive consistent with the Driver Repositioning program.

**Emergency overlap.** If an event coincides with a declared state or local emergency, the price-gouging cap rules in the Weather Operations Policy supersede this policy and the event surge ceiling is suspended.

**Post-event review.** After each major event, operations should compare forecast `request_count` to actual, review the realized multiplier curve, and record pickup ETA performance to improve future pre-positioning recommendations.
