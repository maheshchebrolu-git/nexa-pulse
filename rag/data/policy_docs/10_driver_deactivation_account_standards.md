# Driver Deactivation & Account Standards

**Purpose.** This policy defines the grounds, process, and review path for suspending or deactivating a driver's Nexa Pulse account. Drivers are independent contractors under Proposition 22; this policy governs platform access, not employment.

**Grounds for immediate deactivation.** Egregious conduct results in immediate, permanent loss of access: assault or sexual misconduct, violence, weapons, discrimination, fraud, account sharing, driving under the influence, or a disqualifying background-check result. These require no warning track.

**Grounds for suspension or review.** Lower-severity issues lead to a temporary hold (status forced to `offline`) pending investigation or remediation: expired documents, an open accident report, an excessive cancellation rate, a rating below the deactivation threshold, or an accumulation of credible complaints.

**Decision standard.** To remove bias and ensure consistency, deactivation decisions for serious safety matters follow a standardized decision matrix applied by a dedicated safety-policy review team, rather than the discretion of a single front-line agent. The standard for a permanent deactivation is a confirmed material violation supported by available evidence (GPS data, app timestamps, rider/driver statements, or footage).

**Notice and appeal.** Drivers are notified of a suspension or deactivation, with the reason, generally within 24 hours via email and in-app. Drivers may appeal through a dedicated channel and submit new or updated information; final appeal decisions are communicated within a defined window. Drivers must be afforded any applicable just-cause or advance-notice protections adopted in the operating jurisdiction.

**Operations manager role.** The dashboard shows held and deactivated accounts and the supply impact by zone. Managers do not adjudicate safety deactivations but must account for the lost supply when planning incentives and coverage.
